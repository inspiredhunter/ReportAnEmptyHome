Privacy Notice

Version: 1.2  ·  Last reviewed: 28 August 2026

This notice explains how Property Kickstart uses personal information across its websites, services and tools. It applies to Property with Hanna, the Saturday Supplement newsletter, ReportAnEmptyHome.co.uk and the Property Kickstart EPC Checker.

Brand structure. Property with Hanna, the Saturday Supplement and ReportAnEmptyHome.co.uk are Property Kickstart services. This notice provides one consistent privacy framework across Property Kickstart and its associated websites, forms and tools.

1. Who we are

Property Kickstart is the controller for personal information processed through Property Kickstart services where we decide why and how that information is used. This includes relevant processing carried out through Property with Hanna and ReportAnEmptyHome.co.uk.

For privacy enquiries, contact hello@propertykickstart.co.uk.

2. Where this notice applies

This notice applies when you interact with Property Kickstart through our websites, subdomains, forms, newsletters, property-enquiry journeys, empty-home reporting service, tools or direct communications. This includes services made available through propertykickstart.com, propertykickstart.co.uk, ReportAnEmptyHome.co.uk and relevant Property Kickstart subdomains.

3. Information we may process

Depending on how you interact with us, we may process:

  • information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, telephone number or other contact details;
  • newsletter subscription information, including your email address, optional first name, signup source, signup date and subscription or consent status;
  • information needed to manage email delivery, including confirmation, unsubscribe and suppression records;
  • property-enquiry information, such as a property address or postcode, a description of the situation, preferred contact method and contact details you choose to provide;
  • empty-home reports, including the property address, the reporter's relationship to or knowledge of the property, observations about possible vacancy and related submission metadata;
  • optional photographs, documents or other evidence supplied with an empty-home report;
  • reporter contact details where a reporter chooses to provide them so that we may clarify a report;
  • owner or occupier information obtained from public, licensed or other lawful sources where reasonably necessary to verify a report, identify the relevant owner or consider practical options for bringing a property back into use;
  • records of contact, objections, suppression preferences and responses from owners, reporters or other contacts;
  • communications and service information created when you contact or correspond with us;
  • technical information generated when you use our websites and online services; and
  • for the EPC Checker, the postcode, selected property address, UPRN and EPC certificate information needed to complete the lookup and return the result.

We do not ask community reporters to provide special-category information, family disputes, health information, criminal-history information or other sensitive personal detail. If sensitive information is volunteered, we aim to record only what is genuinely necessary.

4. Why we use personal information and our lawful bases

We use personal information only where we have a lawful basis to do so.

  • Newsletter and related marketing emails: where consent is required, we rely on consent to send the Property with Hanna Saturday Supplement and related email communications you have agreed to receive.
  • Subscription administration: we may use information needed to confirm, record, manage and evidence subscription choices, including double opt-in, unsubscribe and suppression records, based on our legitimate interests in operating a compliant email service and demonstrating those choices.
  • Property enquiries from owners: we use information you provide to understand and respond to your enquiry. Depending on the circumstances, this may be necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract and/or for our legitimate interests in responding to genuine business enquiries.
  • Receiving and assessing empty-home reports: we rely on our legitimate interests in receiving, assessing and verifying information about potentially empty properties, provided those interests are not overridden by the rights and freedoms of affected individuals. We apply safeguards including private processing, data minimisation, cautious treatment of suspected vacancy and controls against malicious or excessive reporting.
  • Owner identification, verification and proportionate property research: we may rely on legitimate interests where necessary to verify a report, establish ownership or understand practical property-related circumstances. We assess necessity and proportionality before using personal information.
  • Owner contact: where we contact an owner about a reported empty property, the lawful basis and communication rules depend on the purpose and channel. Service or transparency communications are treated separately from direct marketing. Postal marketing or certain live calls may in some circumstances rely on legitimate interests, but electronic marketing such as email or SMS is also subject to PECR and will not be sent merely because legitimate interests applies under UK GDPR.
  • EPC Checker: where UK data-protection law applies to EPC lookup information, we rely on our legitimate interests in providing a user-requested property due-diligence tool, operating and securing the service, and diagnosing technical problems.
  • Security, compliance and service operation: we may process limited technical or operational information where necessary for our legitimate interests in maintaining, securing and improving our services, preventing abuse, recording objections and demonstrating compliance.
Your right to object. Where we rely on legitimate interests, you have the right to object to our use of your personal information. Where personal information is used for direct marketing, the right to object is absolute. Contact hello@propertykickstart.co.uk.

5. ReportAnEmptyHome.co.uk

ReportAnEmptyHome.co.uk is a private reporting and property-intelligence service. Reports are not published as a public register and are not made publicly searchable.

A report is treated as information to be assessed, not as proof that a property is empty. We may combine a report with proportionate property or public-source research to verify the situation, identify the relevant owner or decide whether further contact is appropriate.

Reporter identity or contact details are not routinely disclosed to an owner. We may disclose information where required by law, necessary to establish or defend legal rights, or otherwise lawfully required.

Evidence such as photographs is optional. Reporters are asked not to trespass, surveil people, look through windows, confront occupants or collect personal information on our behalf. Photographs should only be taken from places where the reporter is lawfully entitled to be.

6. Information about owners obtained indirectly

Sometimes we may obtain information about an owner or occupier from someone else or from a public, licensed or other lawful source rather than directly from that person. This can include identity and contact information, property ownership information, and relevant property-related information used to assess a report or make contact.

Where Article 14 of the UK GDPR applies, we provide the required privacy information within a reasonable period after obtaining the information and normally no later than one month. If we communicate with the person sooner, we normally provide the information at or before that first communication. Where a lawful exception applies, we document the basis for relying on it.

When appropriate, our first contact will explain that Property Kickstart obtained information indirectly, the categories and source of the information, why we are using it, the lawful basis, relevant retention information and the person's rights.

7. Property with Hanna Saturday Supplement

If you subscribe to the Saturday Supplement, we use the information collected through the signup journey to manage your subscription and send the emails you requested.

Our intended signup process uses a clear, unticked consent choice and double opt-in. We keep appropriate records of when and how a subscription was requested and confirmed.

We do not treat acceptance of this Privacy Notice as consent to marketing. Marketing consent is requested separately.

8. Unsubscribing, objections and suppression

You can unsubscribe from Property with Hanna newsletter and marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link included in those emails or by contacting us.

You can also object to direct marketing at any time. Once an objection or unsubscribe request applies, we keep the minimum information needed on a suppression record so that the preference is not accidentally overridden by later data collection or re-import.

Where an owner asks us not to contact them for marketing, we record and honour that objection across future relevant campaigns and channels.

9. Data minimisation and retention

We aim to collect and retain only the information reasonably needed for the relevant purpose. We review retention rather than keeping information indefinitely simply because storage is available.

  • Active newsletter subscribers: retained while the subscription remains active and as needed to operate it.
  • Unsubscribed or objecting contacts: a limited suppression record may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to honour the preference and demonstrate compliance.
  • Consent records: retained for as long as reasonably necessary to demonstrate how consent or subscription choices were obtained and managed.
  • Property enquiries: retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the enquiry, manage any resulting relationship and meet applicable legal or record-keeping requirements.
  • Invalid or malicious empty-home reports: retained only for a short period sufficient to investigate abuse, prevent repeat misuse and defend relevant claims, then deleted or anonymised where no continuing purpose exists.
  • Unverified inactive empty-home cases: reviewed at around 12 months and deleted or anonymised where there is no continuing legitimate purpose.
  • Active verified cases or owner engagement: retained while genuinely active and then for a documented closure period proportionate to legal, accounting, contractual or claims needs.
  • Evidence files: not retained longer than the case purpose requires.
  • EPC searches: we do not intentionally store EPC postcode, UPRN or selected-address searches in a separate permanent Property Kickstart search-history database.

Technical service providers may retain operational logs for limited periods. Where Property Kickstart deliberately creates an identifiable diagnostic copy to investigate a technical issue, we delete it when the issue is resolved and, unless a longer period is legally required, no later than seven days after creation.

10. Service providers and recipients

We use carefully selected service providers where necessary to operate Property Kickstart and its services. Depending on the service, these may include:

  • secure data-storage and evidence-storage providers;
  • email delivery and contact-management providers;
  • website hosting and technical infrastructure providers; and
  • official or licensed data-source providers used to return or verify property information.

We require service providers to process personal information only for authorised purposes and subject to appropriate contractual, security and data-protection safeguards. Private empty-home report narratives, photographs and case intelligence are kept within the systems intended for that purpose and are not used as public datasets.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

11. International processing

Some service providers may process information outside the UK. Where this happens and UK data-protection law requires safeguards, we use or require an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism or other recognised safeguard. We review processor and international-transfer arrangements as part of our service governance.

12. Analytics and cookies

The EPC Checker currently does not use visitor analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies. Essential technical processing by hosting infrastructure may still occur.

Other Property Kickstart services may use different technical features. Where non-essential analytics, tracking or cookies are introduced, we review the relevant privacy and cookie information and obtain consent where required before enabling them.

13. EPC Checker data source and purpose

The EPC Checker uses the official Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government Energy Performance Certificate data service for England and Wales to help users retrieve official EPC information for property research and due diligence.

Property Kickstart does not use the EPC Checker to create a permanent public history of searches.

14. Accuracy and correction

Information about a potentially empty property may be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. A report is not treated as confirmation of vacancy. Where an affected person tells us information is wrong, we consider the correction promptly and may restrict, amend or remove the disputed information where appropriate.

EPC information is sourced from the official government dataset. Property Kickstart does not create the underlying certificate record and cannot guarantee that an official record reflects the current condition of a property.

15. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

You may also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

To exercise a right, contact hello@propertykickstart.co.uk. We may need enough information to identify the relevant data and verify your identity where appropriate.

16. Security

We use proportionate technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. ReportAnEmptyHome evidence storage is intended to remain private. Access is restricted, and sensitive report operations are handled through controlled technical processes designed to prevent unauthorised access.

17. Changes to this notice

We review this notice when our services, legal obligations or data-processing arrangements change. We update the review date when the notice is materially reviewed. Where a new use of personal information requires updated privacy information to be brought to affected individuals' attention, we do so before that new use begins where required.
