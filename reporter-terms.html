Reporting an empty home safely and honestly

Version: 1.0  ·  Last reviewed: 28 August 2026

ReportAnEmptyHome.co.uk accepts private information about homes that may be empty. A report helps us decide what, if anything, should be checked. It is not proof that a property is empty.

Your declaration

By submitting a report, you confirm that the information you provide is honest and is based on what you have personally observed, know or reasonably believe. You also confirm that you have followed these Reporter Terms when gathering or submitting any information or evidence.

Please keep yourself and others safe

  • Do not trespass or enter land or buildings without permission.
  • Do not look through windows, monitor occupants or carry out surveillance.
  • Do not confront owners, occupiers, neighbours or anyone else.
  • Do not investigate an owner's personal information on our behalf.
  • Only take photographs from places where you are lawfully entitled to be.
  • Avoid photographing people, vehicle number plates or private interiors unless genuinely necessary and lawful.

What to include

Please give us only information that is relevant to why you think the property may be empty. Do not submit sensitive personal information such as health information, criminal history, family disputes or other private matters.

How we treat reports

Reports are private and are not published or made publicly searchable. We assess information before drawing any conclusion about a property. Reporter contact details are not routinely disclosed to an owner. We may correct, restrict, remove or disregard information where it appears inaccurate, excessive, unsafe, malicious or no longer necessary.

Important: Malicious, knowingly false or abusive reports may be rejected and excluded from any assessment.

For information about how personal data is handled, read our Privacy Notice.
