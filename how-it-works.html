How it works

A simple, private way to tell us about an empty home.

1. Tell us about the property

Owners can tell us about their own property. Other people can tell us about a property they believe may be empty.

2. We review the information

A report is information for us to assess. It is not treated as proof that a property is empty.

3. We explore what happens next

Where appropriate, we may contact an owner to help them understand their situation and explore their options.
