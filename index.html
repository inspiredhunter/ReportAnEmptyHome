    Owner introduction letter

    An empty property can quietly cost you more than you realise.

    Council tax, insurance and ongoing upkeep can continue month after month, while an unused property can become more difficult and expensive to manage over time.

    What you may not know is whether keeping it empty is costing you more than it needs to, or what other possibilities you could be missing.

    Before you decide what happens next, it may be worth understanding what your property could be costing you now and what options you may not yet have considered.

    You do not need to know what you want to do with the property. You do not need to have made any decisions.

    It starts with understanding what is possible.

    We can help you clarify your situation and understand and compare the options available to you, so you can decide what makes the most sense for your property and your circumstances.

    The property may be standing still. Your options don’t have to be.

    Warm regards,
    Hanna
